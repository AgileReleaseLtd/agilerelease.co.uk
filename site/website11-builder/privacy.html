Privacy Your details

Your details

Short version: this website doesn't collect or keep anything about you.

Asking for a quote

When you fill in the quote form, the website writes your answers as a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your address or anything else you type.

Once you send it, we see your message, your number or email address, and any photos you attach, like any other message. We use them only to arrange a visit, prepare your quote and, if you go ahead, to run your job.

How long we keep things

If you don't go ahead, we delete your messages and photos after twelve months. If we build for you, we keep the drawings, certificates and job records for as long as our guarantee lasts, plus one year, because you or a future owner may need them.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@kemberanddaughter.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
