03 How we work

One team from the first visit to the guarantee

You deal with Ray or Nina the whole way through. No sales people, and no job handed on to someone you've never met.

  1. 01

    Visit and quote

    We come round, measure up and talk budget. A written, itemised quote follows within ten working days.

  2. 02

    Drawings

    Nina draws the house as it is and as it will be. Change it as often as you like before we fix the design.

  3. 03

    Permissions

    Planning or a lawful development certificate, building control, party wall notices. We do the paperwork.

  4. 04

    The build

    One of us on site every day, a tidy site, and a WhatsApp update with photos every Friday.

  5. 05

    Sign-off

    Building control's completion certificate, the electrical and gas certificates, and a snag list we work through.

  6. 06

    Guarantee

    Ten years on structural work, two on everything else, and a return visit after six months.

    06 Questions

    What people ask us first

    Anything else? Message us and Nina or Ray will reply.

    Do you do the drawings and the planning application?

    Yes. Nina trained as an architectural technologist and draws every job: the house as it is, and as it will be. We apply for planning or a lawful development certificate, book building control and serve the party wall notices. For a listed building or a very large project we're happy to work with your architect instead.

    How long will it take?

    On site: a loft takes 8 to 12 weeks, an extension 10 to 16, a kitchen 3 to 6 and a bathroom 2 to 3. Before that, allow two to four months for drawings and permissions. See a typical timeline.

    Can we stay in the house while you work?

    Usually, yes. For a loft we work from scaffolding outside and only open up the stairs near the end. For a kitchen extension we set up a temporary kitchen in another room with a sink, a fridge and a hob.

    What if you find something unexpected?

    Old houses hide things: rotten joists, old drains, a wall that isn't what it seems. We show you, explain the options and give you a written price before doing any extra work. Your quote also lists the parts we can't price until we open up, with an allowance for each.

    Do your prices include VAT?

    Yes. Every price on this website and in our quotes includes VAT at 20%.

    Who does the electrics and the gas?

    Our own team does the building work. Electrics are done by a registered electrician and gas work by a Gas Safe registered engineer, both people we've worked with for years. You get their certificates at the end.

    What hours do you work on site?

    8am to 6pm on weekdays and 8am to 1pm on Saturdays, never on Sundays or bank holidays. Those are the hours councils usually allow for noisy building work. We tell the neighbours before the noisiest days.
