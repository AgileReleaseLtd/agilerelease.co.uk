    How it works

    From enquiry to first toast

    1. 1

      Send an enquiry

      Tell us the date, how many of you and what you are celebrating. It takes two minutes.

    2. 2

      We hold the date

      Levan replies within a day with a quote, and holds the room for you for three days.

    3. 3

      Pay the deposit

      £20 a head, by a secure card link. It comes off the final bill.

    4. 4

      Final numbers

      Confirm your numbers and dietary needs 72 hours before. Then just turn up hungry.

    The small print

    • If you cancel more than 14 days before, we refund the deposit in full. Between 7 and 14 days, we refund half. Within 7 days, we keep it.
    • You pay for the final numbers you confirm 72 hours before, even if fewer people come on the night.
    • If the bill for food and drink is below the minimum spend, the difference is added as a room charge.
    • An optional service charge of 12.5% is added to the bill, all of it for the team.
