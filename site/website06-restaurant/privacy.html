Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Booking a table, a feast or a voucher

When you send a booking request, a group enquiry or a voucher request, the website writes it as a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your notes or your number.

Once you send it, we see your message and your number or email address, like any other message. We use them only to reply, to confirm your booking and to contact you about it. If you tell us about an allergy or a dietary need, we use that only to cook for you safely and share it only with the kitchen. We delete booking chats after twelve months.

Deposits and payments

When a booking needs a deposit, we send you a secure link from our card payment provider. Your card details go to them, never to us or to this website.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to table@jonjoli.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
