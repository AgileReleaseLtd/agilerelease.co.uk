    Switching accountant

    Moving to us takes about three weeks. You do very little of it.

    You can switch at any time of year, even in January. We contact your current accountant for you, so there is no awkward conversation.

    1. 1

      A free 20-minute call

      We find out what you need, and confirm a fixed monthly fee in writing.

    2. 2

      Engagement letter and ID check

      You sign our engagement letter. We check your ID, as every accountant must under the money laundering rules.

    3. 3

      Professional clearance

      We write to your current accountant. They confirm there is no professional reason we should not act for you, and send the handover: recent returns, figures carried forward and anything open with HMRC.

    4. 4

      Access to HMRC

      You authorise us as your agent with HMRC. For a company, we also ask for its Companies House authentication code.

    5. 5

      We take it from there

      We check what is due, put every deadline in our diary and tell you what we need from you, and by when.

    If your current accountant is still owed fees, it is best to settle them, but unpaid fees do not stop you moving. Behind on returns? We do catch-up work too.

    Questions

    Asked most often

    Anything else? Send us a message and we will reply within one working day.

    This is general information about UK tax, not advice for your situation. Your own figures can change the answer.

    Do I need an accountant as a sole trader?

    No, the law does not require one, and plenty of people file their own return. People come to us when the return takes them a weekend, when they are not sure what they can claim, when Making Tax Digital starts for them, or when they would simply rather not think about it.

    What does "plus VAT" mean on your fees?

    We are registered for VAT, so we add 20% to our fees. A £40 monthly fee is £48 with VAT. If your business is VAT-registered, you can usually claim that VAT back.

    What is a payment on account?

    If your last Self Assessment bill was £1,000 or more, HMRC usually asks for two advance payments towards the next one, each half of last year's bill: one on 31 January and one on 31 July. They do not apply if 80% or more of your tax was already taken at source, for example through PAYE. If your income has dropped, we can ask HMRC to reduce them.

    I have missed the 31 January deadline. What now?

    File as soon as you can. Penalties start at £100 for a late return and grow the longer it stays late, and HMRC charges interest on tax paid late. Send us what you have and we will usually turn a late return round within a week.

    Can I switch to you in the middle of the year?

    Yes. We ask your current accountant for professional clearance and a handover, and you authorise us with HMRC. It takes about three weeks. How switching works.

    Do you work with my software, or my spreadsheet?

    Most likely. We work in most HMRC-recognised software. If you keep records in a spreadsheet, bridging software can send your Making Tax Digital updates from it.

    Do I have to come to the office?

    No. Most clients never visit: we work by phone, video call, email and WhatsApp, and you send records through a secure upload link. You are welcome to come in, though, and the kettle is on.

    Is the first call really free?

    Yes. A 20-minute call or meeting, with no charge and no obligation. If we can help, you get a fixed fee in writing afterwards.
