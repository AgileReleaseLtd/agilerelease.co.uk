Dates that depend on you

Three more to know about

  • 1m + 7d

    VAT returns

    Your VAT return and payment are due one month and seven days after the end of each VAT period.

  • 30 days

    Registering for VAT

    Once your VAT-taxable sales go over £90,000 in any 12 months, register within 30 days of the end of that month.

  • 60 days

    Selling a second home or a let property

    If there is Capital Gains Tax to pay, report it and pay it within 60 days of completion.
