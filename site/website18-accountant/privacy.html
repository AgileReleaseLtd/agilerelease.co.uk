Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

The enquiry form, the package finder and the checkers

Everything you type into this website stays on your own device. The package finder, the Making Tax Digital checker and the company dates are worked out in your browser. The enquiry form writes your answers as a WhatsApp message (or an email) on your device, and nothing is sent until you press send yourself.

When you contact us

Once you send a message, we see what you wrote and your number or email address, like any other message. We use them only to reply and, if you become a client, to do your work.

If you become a client, the law requires us to check your identity and keep our records for some years, for anti-money laundering and tax purposes. Our engagement letter explains what we keep and for how long. If you do not become a client, we delete your enquiry within twelve months.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@quarterday.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
