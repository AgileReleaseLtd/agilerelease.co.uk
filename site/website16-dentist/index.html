Nervous patients

If the dentist worries you, tell us.

Lots of our patients felt the same before their first visit. Nobody here will rush you or tell you off for how long it has been. These are the things we do as standard.

  • A stop signal

    Raise your hand and we stop. Every time, no questions.

  • Nothing without explaining

    We tell you what we are about to do before we do it, and you can ask us to slow down.

  • A longer first visit

    Forty minutes, so it can be a chat and a look, and nothing more if that is what you want.

  • Numbing gel first

    Before any injection, so you feel very little. We wait until you are properly numb.

  • Your own music

    Bring headphones, or borrow ours. A blanket and sunglasses for the light, too.

  • A quiet wait

    Ask for the first appointment of the day and walk straight in, with no waiting room.

We do not offer sedation at the practice. If you need it, we can refer you to a specialist sedation service and keep seeing you for everything else.

Before your visit

A minute of slow breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold for 2, and out for 6. Four rounds take under a minute. Try it now, or in the waiting room.

New patients

Joining takes three steps

Dental practices in England do not register NHS patients the way GP surgeries do. Joining means we add you to our patient list and book your first visit.

  1. 1

    Send us your details

    A short form: your name, date of birth, how to reach you, and whether you want NHS or private care. No health details.

  2. 2

    We call you back

    Usually the same working day. We talk through what you need and book a time that suits you.

  3. 3

    Your first visit

    You fill in a medical history form in private, then have a check-up and leave with a written plan and prices.

Accepting new NHS patients No

NHS places for new patients are limited. We are not taking new adult NHS patients at the moment, but children under 18 are welcome on the NHS. Adults can join our NHS waiting list, or be seen privately in the meantime.

Questions

Things people ask us

Anything else? Call or message us and we will help.

Are you taking new NHS patients?

Not adults at the moment, sorry. NHS places for new patients are limited. Children under 18 are welcome on the NHS. Adults can join our NHS waiting list, be seen privately in the meantime, or look for another NHS dentist on nhs.uk.

It has been years since I saw a dentist. Will I be told off?

No. We see people every week who have stayed away for a long time, often because of a bad experience. We start with a look and a chat, and we go at your pace from there.

Can I have NHS check-ups and some private treatment?

Yes. Many of our patients do. We always tell you what is available on the NHS and what is private, with the prices, before you decide. The choice is yours.

How do I know what my treatment will cost?

After your check-up you get a written plan with the cost of each item. NHS treatment is one of three national charges. Private treatment is priced on our price list, and nothing is added without asking you first.

Do you see children?

Yes, from their first tooth. NHS dental care is free for under-18s, and for under-19s in full-time education. You can book them in on the same day as your own check-up.

Do you offer sedation?

Not at the practice. If you need sedation, we can refer you to a specialist service nearby, and keep seeing you here for everything else.

What if I need to cancel?

Please let us know at least 24 hours before. We keep a short list of people who want an earlier appointment, often because they are in pain, and we can offer them your time.

How do I pay?

By card or contactless at reception, after each appointment. For private treatment over £1,000 you can pay in stages as the treatment goes along.
