How our emergency slots work

Held back today

Every weekday we hold back four appointments for emergencies, and two on Saturday mornings.

    A slot shown here may already have been given to someone. Call to check: we tell you straight away if we can see you today.

    Our emergency policy

    • Call the emergency line from 8am on weekdays, or 9am on Saturdays. We ask a few questions to judge how urgent it is.
    • Slots go in order of need: severe pain, swelling and injuries first.
    • Our own patients come first. If you are not our patient, we still help when we can, on the NHS or privately.
    • The aim is to stop pain and treat infection. Any further treatment is booked for another day.

    A knocked-out adult tooth

    1. Hold it by the white top, not the root.
    2. If it is dirty, rinse it briefly in milk.
    3. Push it back into the socket if you can, and bite on a clean cloth.
    4. If not, keep it in milk and see a dentist straight away.

    Never put a baby tooth back in.
