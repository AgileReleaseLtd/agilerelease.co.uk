Join as a new patient

Send us a few details and we will call you back, usually the same working day, to book your first visit. It takes about two minutes, and there are no health questions here.

Accepting new NHS patients No Under-18s welcome. Adults can join the waiting list.
Accepting new NHS patients No

NHS places for new patients are limited. We are not taking new adult NHS patients at the moment, but children under 18 are welcome on the NHS. Adults can join our NHS waiting list, or be seen privately in the meantime.

What happens next

  1. We call you back at a time that suits you.
  2. We book your first visit: 40 minutes if you are nervous.
  3. At the visit you fill in a medical history form in private.
  4. You have a check-up and leave with a written plan and prices.

Bring with you

  • A list of any medicines you take
  • Proof if you get free NHS dental care
  • Your last dentist's name, if you know it

Rather talk?

Call reception on 020 7946 0163. In pain today? See emergency appointments.
