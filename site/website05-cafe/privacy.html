Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Orders and saving a spot

When you order ahead or save a spot at an event, the website writes your message on your own device and opens WhatsApp. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your order or your number.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them only to confirm your order or your spot and to contact you about it. We delete these chats after six months.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts and maps. The map is a drawing; the ‘Open in Google Maps’ and ‘Open in Apple Maps’ links only take you to those sites if you press them.

In the café

We have a small security camera over the till, with a sign by the door. Its recordings are kept for 14 days and then overwritten. The wifi does not ask for your details.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp, at the counter or by email to hello@cobnut.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
