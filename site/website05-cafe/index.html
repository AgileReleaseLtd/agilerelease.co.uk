Hello from Cobnut

A small café with a big kitchen table

Ros grew up in Kent, where cobnuts grow in old orchards and end up in everything from flapjacks to brownies. Dele spent ten years making coffee in Bermondsey. In 2019 they took over an empty shop on Wickfield Parade and opened the café they wanted on their own street.

We bake every morning and make the soup from scratch. There is a big table for sharing, a window seat for watching the world go by, and nobody minds if you stay for a second coffee.

Good to know

Bring the dog, the laptop or the buggy

The small things people ask before they come.

  • Dogs welcome

    Inside and out. There is a water bowl by the door and a jar of biscuits on the counter. Please keep them off the chairs.

  • Free wifi

    The password is on the counter. Laptops are welcome on weekdays; at weekends the big table is laptop-free from 10am to 2pm.

  • Step-free way in

    A level entrance with a wide door, and room for a wheelchair at the front tables. Our toilet is on the ground floor but is not fully accessible.

  • Children

    High chairs, a baby-changing table, small portions and babyccinos. Buggies fit, though weekend mornings get busy.

  • Card or cash

    We take cards, phones and cash, with no minimum spend.

  • Bring your own cup

    25p off any hot drink in your own cup. Or borrow one of ours and bring it back next time.

Getting here

  • By trainHerne Hill station is a four-minute walk: Southeastern trains from Victoria and Brixton, and Thameslink from Blackfriars and Elephant & Castle.

  • From the TubeFrom Brixton (Victoria line), take the 3, 37 or 196 bus to Herne Hill, or walk 25 minutes through Brockwell Park.

  • By busThe 3, 37, 68, 196, 201 and 468 stop at Herne Hill station. Walk east along Half Moon Lane and turn left into Wickfield Parade.

  • By bike or carTwo bike stands right outside. Street parking nearby is pay-by-phone on weekdays, so the bus is usually easier.

    Questions

    Things people ask us

    Anything else? Send us a message and we will get back to you.

    Can I book a table?

    Not in the daytime: tables are first come, first served, and they turn over quickly. For eight or more, message us a day ahead and we will push tables together. For events, use the button on What's on.

    I have a food allergy. Can you help?

    Yes. Tell us before you order and we will show you which of the 14 main allergens are in each dish. Our small kitchen uses nuts (we are called Cobnut, after all), gluten, milk and eggs, so we cannot promise anything is free of traces.

    Do you have oat milk? Does it cost extra?

    Oat and soya milk cost nothing extra. Our oat milk is not certified gluten-free, so choose soya if that matters to you.

    Do you make whole cakes to order?

    Yes, with 48 hours' notice. A 20cm cake serves 10 to 12 and costs from £34. Message us with the cake, the day and any writing on top.

    Can I hire the café for a party?

    In the evenings, yes: up to 30 people seated or 45 standing, from £150 for three hours plus food. Message us with a date and we will send you a menu.

    Can I order for a meeting or an office?

    Yes. Send us your order the day before for anything over ten drinks or a tray of bakes, and we will have it boxed up and ready to collect.
