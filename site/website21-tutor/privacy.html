Privacy

Your details, and your child's

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you or your child.

Booking a free trial

When you fill in the trial form, the website writes your request as a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your number or your child's name.

We ask for as little as we can: your name, a phone number, an email address if you want to give one, and your child's first name and school year. Please do not send medical details or school reports through the form.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them only to arrange the trial and, if your child joins, to run their lessons.

If your child joins

We keep a short record for each pupil: their name, school year, exam board, attendance, test scores, reports, and the contact details of the parents or carers you tell us about. Only the tutors who teach your child and the centre office can see it.

We keep it while your child has lessons with us and for one year after they leave, then delete it, unless we must keep something for longer by law, such as invoices or a safeguarding record.

Tools on this page

The fee calculator, the school-year helper and the online lesson checklist work only inside your browser. What you choose is not sent anywhere and is forgotten when you leave the page.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@markwell.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
