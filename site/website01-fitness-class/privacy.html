Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Booking a class

When you book, the website writes your booking as a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your note or your number.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them only to confirm your booking and to contact you about classes you have booked. We delete booking chats after twelve months.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@ironfern.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
