Questions

Before your first class

Anything else? Send us a message and we will reply.

I have never done a class. Which one should I start with?

Foundations or Mat Pilates. Tell us it is your first time when you book, and the coach will show you around and keep an eye on you.

What do I need to bring?

Trainers, a water bottle and a towel. Yoga and Pilates are done barefoot. We provide mats, blocks, boxing gloves and hand wraps.

How do I book, and how do I know I have a place?

Pick a class on the timetable and send the booking on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm your place, usually within the hour while the studio is open.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Message us up to six hours before the class. Later than that, a class pass loses the class. Drop-ins pay on arrival, so there is nothing to refund.

Is there anywhere to shower and change?

Two showers, changing rooms and lockers. Bring a padlock, or borrow one from the front desk.

I have an injury or a health condition. Can I still come?

Usually, yes. Tell the coach before the class so they can suggest other moves. If you are not sure, check with your GP first.
