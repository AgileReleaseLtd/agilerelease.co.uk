Fragile items

The breakable things, handled with care

Most breakages happen in badly packed boxes, not in the van. This is how we pack the things that matter.

  • Plates and bowls

    Each one wrapped in paper and stood on its edge, never flat. Small boxes only, so they're never too heavy.

  • Glasses

    In a divider box, one glass to a cell. Stems wrapped twice. The box is marked fragile and this way up.

  • Pictures and mirrors

    Corner protectors, bubble wrap, then a picture box. They travel standing up, against a mattress.

  • TVs and screens

    In the original box if you kept it, or our TV box with foam corners. Cables bagged and taped to the back.

  • Lamps and shades

    Shades packed on their own so nothing presses on them. Bulbs out, bases wrapped, flexes tied.

  • Odd shapes

    Plants in open-top boxes, loaded last and out first. Anything that mustn't tip is marked this way up.

Antiques, artwork or a piano? Tell us before the day. We'll plan how to move it, or bring in a specialist.
