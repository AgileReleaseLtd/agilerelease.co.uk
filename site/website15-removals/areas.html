    London moves and longer moves

    Same crews, same care. The difference is how the travel is charged.

    Inside London

    • By the hour, from when we arrive to when we finish, with a two-hour minimum.
    • A flat fee for getting the van to you: free in our home patch, £25 across London, £45 in outer London.
    • No charge for the drive back to the yard.
    • The Congestion Charge, £18, is passed on at cost if either address is in the zone. Our vans meet the ULEZ rules, so there's nothing to pay for that.

    Out of London

    • By the hour for the job itself, driving included. No shared loads: we go straight there.
    • The empty van's drive back to London at half rate.
    • Fuel and road costs at 45p a mile on any leg over 25 miles.
    • Over 150 miles the crew stays the night (£60 each), and we'd usually agree a fixed price instead.
