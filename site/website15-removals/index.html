How a move day works

No surprises on the day

The same crew from the first box to the last, and one crew lead in charge who you can talk to.

  1. Before

    A video call or a list

    Ten minutes on a video call, or send a list and a few photos. We confirm the price, the van and the crew in writing.

  2. The day before

    Packing, if you booked it

    For bigger homes our packers come the day before. Your crew lead texts to confirm the arrival time.

  3. 8:00am

    Arrive and walk round

    We walk through every room with you, then put down floor runners and door protectors.

  4. 8:30am

    Wrap and load

    Furniture in blankets, sofas and mattresses in covers, beds taken apart. Heavy things go in first.

  5. On the road

    Straight there

    No other jobs on the way. You get a message when we're fifteen minutes from the new place.

  6. Unload

    Room by room

    Boxes go to the room on their label. Furniture goes where you want it, not just through the door.

  7. Finish

    Beds up, last look

    We rebuild what we took apart, do a last walk-round with you, and you pay by card or bank transfer.

  8. How long it takes

    Most one-bedroom flats take four to five hours, door to door. A three-bedroom house is a full day.

Questions

Good to know before you book

Anything else? Send us a message and we'll reply the same day in office hours.

How close will the online estimate be?

Usually within the range it shows. Distances come from postcode districts, not a route planner, and every home is different. A ten-minute video call or a list from you lets us fix the price before the day.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks for most moves. Fridays, Saturdays and the last few days of the month go first, because that's when house sales complete. Small moves can often be done in a day or two.

What if the keys are late on completion day?

It happens. The first 30 minutes of waiting are free. After that, waiting time is charged at the normal hourly rate, by the half hour. We'll keep you posted, and we can often start loading while you wait.

Do I need to empty drawers and wardrobes?

Light clothes can stay in drawers if the furniture is solid. Empty anything heavy or fragile. Hanging clothes go into wardrobe boxes, which we can bring.

Can you move a piano, a safe or an American fridge?

Upright pianos and big fridges, yes, with an extra mover. Send us a photo and the stairs at both ends first. For grand pianos and safes we'll put you in touch with a specialist.

What won't you move?

Gas bottles, petrol, paint thinners and other things that can catch fire, and anything alive except house plants. Cash, jewellery and important papers should travel with you.

Who sorts out parking?

Tell us what the parking is like at both ends. If the van needs a bay suspension or a visitor permit, we can apply to the council for you. Some councils need two weeks' notice.

Can I change the date or cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to seven days before. After that we keep the £75 deposit, because we'll have turned other work away. Small moves don't need a deposit.
