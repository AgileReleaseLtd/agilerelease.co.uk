Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website doesn't collect or keep anything about you.

Estimates, bookings and box orders

The estimate calculator, the booking form and the box shop all work inside your own browser. When you press send, the website writes a WhatsApp message (or an email) on your device. Nothing goes anywhere until you send it yourself, and this website never receives or stores your postcodes, your list, your name or your number.

Once you send it, we see your message and your number, like any other message. We use them to price and plan your move, to confirm the booking, and to contact you about it. We keep booking chats and invoices for six years, because the tax rules ask us to, and delete other enquiries after twelve months.

The moving checklist

If you tick things off on the moving checklist, your ticks and your moving date are saved in your own browser (its local storage), so they're still there next time. They never leave your device. Press Clear ticks, or clear your browser's site data, to remove them.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts and maps.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@hitherandthither.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
