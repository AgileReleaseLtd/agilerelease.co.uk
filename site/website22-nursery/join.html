    Settling in

    Three gentle visits, at no charge

    In the two weeks before your start date, your child visits with you a few times, so their key person and the room are familiar before the first full day.

    1. An hour together

      You stay with your child. Their key person plays alongside and asks you all about home: sleeps, feeds, comforters, words and favourite songs.

    2. Two hours, with a coffee break

      You settle your child, then pop out for half an hour or so. We call you if they need you, and we tell you honestly how it went.

    3. A morning, with lunch

      A morning session with lunch, as on a normal day. Most children are ready after this, and some need a few more visits. That is fine, and still free.

    On the first few days, start with shorter days if you can, and keep goodbyes short and cheerful. Ring us as often as you like: we always answer.

    What to bring

    A small bag, all labelled

    Your child gets a peg and a basket of their own. Please put their name on everything: it all looks the same at 5:45pm.

    • Two sets of spare clothesIncluding socks and pants. Play here is messy.
    • Wellies and a waterproof suitWe go out in all weathers. They can stay on your child's peg.
    • A sun hat or a warm hatDepending on the season. We provide sun cream, or send your own.
    • A comforter for sleepA favourite toy, blanket or dummy, if your child has one.
    • Milk for babiesFormula or expressed milk, and bottles, all labelled. We sterilise them here.
    • Any medicinesIn the original box, with a form you sign at the door.

    Nappies, wipes and food are optional extras from us, or bring your own. See optional extras.
