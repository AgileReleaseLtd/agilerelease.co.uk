Privacy

Your details, and your child's

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you. What you send us, we keep safe and only use to look after your child.

The waiting-list form

When you fill in the waiting-list form, the website writes your details as a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app, and the website itself never receives or stores your child's name, their date of birth or your contact details.

Once you send it, we see your message and your number or email address, like any other message. We use them only to manage the waiting list, arrange a show-round and offer you a place. If your child does not take up a place, we delete waiting-list details within 12 months.

When your child starts

We keep the records the law asks of a nursery: contact and emergency details, health needs and allergies, attendance, accidents and medicines, and notes on your child's learning and development. We keep them securely, share them only with staff who need them (and, where the law requires, with the local council, health professionals or Ofsted), and keep them only as long as the law says.

Photos

We take photos of children only on nursery tablets, and only with your written consent. They are shared with you privately through our parent app, and never posted online.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts. The fee calculator works out your estimate in your own browser.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we do not need to keep, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@buttontin.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
