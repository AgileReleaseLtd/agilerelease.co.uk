What children learn

The seven areas of learning

Every nursery in England follows the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS). It sets out seven areas of learning and development. The three prime areas come first, because everything else is built on them. Children learn all seven through play, conversation and everyday routines, not worksheets.

Food and allergies

Cooked here, every morning

Desmond cooks breakfast, lunch and tea in our kitchen from fresh ingredients, following the government's nutrition guidance for early years. Low in salt and sugar, fruit and vegetables every day, and water always within reach.

Lunch this week

A three-week menu that changes with the seasons. Vegetarian versions of every dish.

  • MondayChicken and sweet potato casserole with rice
  • TuesdayLentil and vegetable shepherd's pie
  • WednesdaySalmon fishcakes, peas and new potatoes
  • ThursdayBean chilli with jacket potato and yoghurt
  • FridayVegetable risotto with cheese

  • Allergies and intolerances

    Before your child starts we ask about allergies, intolerances and special diets, and write an allergy plan with you and, if needed, your GP. Every member of staff knows it, and at each meal one named adult checks every plate against it.

  • No nuts, and every dish labelled

    We do not cook with nuts. Desmond labels every dish for the 14 main allergens, and you can see the week's full menu and labels on the board in the hall.

  • Weaning at your baby's pace

    We talk with you about the textures your baby knows and follow what you do at home: purées, mashed or finger food. We never move on just because of age.

  • Every family's food

    Vegetarian, vegan, halal, dairy-free and other diets are no trouble. Tell us what your family eats and we will plan around it.

  • Safe eating

    Children sit down to eat, with a grown-up sitting with them. Food is cut to prevent choking, and a member of staff with paediatric first aid is in the room at every meal.

  • Food from home is fine

    Food from us is an optional extra. You can send a packed meal instead: we keep it in the fridge and serve it at the table with everyone else.
