    We bake with gluten, eggs, milk and nuts

    Questions

    Good to know

    Anything else? Send us a message and we will reply.

    Can you keep a loaf back for me?

    Yes. Message us by 4pm the day before and we will put it aside until noon. You pay when you collect.

    Can I order a cake for this weekend?

    We need 5 days' notice for most cakes and 14 days for tiered ones, so the order form only offers days we can do. If you are in a hurry, message us: there is sometimes a spare 6 inch cake in the shop.

    Do you deliver?

    Not bread or everyday cakes. We deliver and set up tiered wedding cakes within 8 miles of the shop for £35. Everything else is collected from the shop.

    Is there anything for coeliacs?

    We are sorry, but no. We make sponges without gluten, but in the same kitchen as wheat flour, so we cannot promise they are free from traces.

    Do you sell coffee? Can I sit in?

    Filter coffee and tea to take away. The shop is small, so there is no seating inside, but there is a bench outside the window.

    How can I pay?

    Card, contactless or cash in the shop. Cake deposits are paid by a secure card link we send you on WhatsApp or by email.
