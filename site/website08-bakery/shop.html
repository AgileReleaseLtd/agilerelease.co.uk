Allergens

The 14 allergens, and the four we use

UK food law names 14 allergens. We bake with four of them, in one small kitchen.

Any of our food may contain traces of gluten, eggs, milk and nuts. We clean down between batches, but flour gets everywhere. Our made-without-gluten and vegan cakes are made in the same kitchen, so they are not suitable for coeliacs or for severe allergies.

Every label in the shop lists the allergens in that item. Ask to see the full ingredients and we will show you, including the labels on anything we buy in, such as chocolate and sprinkles.

Used in our kitchen

  • Cereals containing gluten
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Tree nuts

Not in our recipes

    Not in our recipes means we do not add it. A few bought-in ingredients carry their own "may contain" warnings, which we can show you.
