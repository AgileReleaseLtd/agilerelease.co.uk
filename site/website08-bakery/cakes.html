          How ordering works

          Deposits, changes and cancellations

          The plain version, so there are no surprises.

          1. 1

            You send your order

            On WhatsApp or by email, with a photo if you have one. Nothing is booked yet.

          2. 2

            We reply with a price

            Within one working day: the final price, any questions, and a secure link to pay the deposit.

          3. 3

            You pay half

            Your date and slot are held once the deposit is paid. Until then, the date is not saved for you.

          4. 4

            You collect

            Pay the rest in the shop when you collect, by card, contactless or cash.

          Deposit
          Half the price, paid within 48 hours of our reply. If it is not paid by then, we may give the slot to someone else.
          Changes
          Wording, colours and decoration can change up to 3 days before collection. The size or flavour can change up to 7 days before, if we can still do it. We will tell you if the price changes.
          Cancelling
          More than 14 days before collection: the deposit back in full. 7 to 14 days before: half the deposit back. Less than 7 days: we keep the deposit, as the ingredients are bought and the time is booked.
          Collection
          In the slot you chose. Bring something flat for the car: a cake travels best in the footwell or on a level boot floor, not on a seat.

          Cake questions

          Before you order

          Not sure? Message us and we will help you choose.

          How many people will my cake feed?

          Each size shows its party portions, about 2.5 cm wide. For a wedding or a big buffet, cut into fingers and a cake goes about twice as far.

          Can each tier be a different flavour?

          Yes, at no extra cost. Choose one flavour in the form and tell us the others in the notes.

          Do you make cakes shaped like characters or numbers?

          We stick to round cakes and do them well. We can add gold number candles, a theme in the colours, or piped decoration that suits it.

          Do you deliver wedding cakes?

          Yes. We deliver and set up tiered cakes within 8 miles of the shop for £35. Tell us the venue in the notes and we will add it to the price.

          How do I keep the cake until the party?

          In its box, somewhere cool, out of the sun. Buttercream cakes are best eaten at room temperature within 3 days. Cakes with fresh fruit or cream cheese go in the fridge, out an hour before serving.

          Can we try the flavours first?

          For wedding cakes, yes: a tasting box of four flavours is £18, taken off the price if you book. For other cakes, the same sponges are in the shop by the slice.
