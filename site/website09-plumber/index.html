Our guarantee

If it goes wrong, we come back

Every job we do is guaranteed for 12 months. If something we fixed or fitted fails in that time, we come back and put it right at no charge. Parts also carry the maker's own warranty, and new boilers come with up to 10 years from the maker when they are serviced every year.

  • The price is agreed first. If a job turns out bigger, we stop and ask before we carry on.

  • The paperwork is done. Service records, gas safety certificates and Building Regulations notices, sent by email.

Gas Safe

Check who is at your door

By law, anyone who works on gas in your home must be on the Gas Safe Register. Every Pinn gas engineer carries a Gas Safe ID card. Ask to see it, and check three things:

  1. 1 The photo matches the person at your door.
  2. 2 The licence number and dates. Check the number on the Gas Safe Register website, or call them free on 0800 408 5500.
  3. 3 The back of the card lists the gas work they are qualified to do, such as boilers or cookers. Make sure your job is on it.

Questions

Good questions to ask a plumber

Anything else? Ask us. We would rather explain than surprise you.

Is there a call-out fee?

No separate one. The first hour's price covers getting to you and the first hour of work. After that we charge by the half hour, and parts are extra at cost. Postcodes in our outer area add £20 for travel.

How quickly can you get here in an emergency?

In Pinner, Eastcote, Ruislip, Northwood and North Harrow, usually within the hour; further out, within 90 minutes to two hours. If we cannot make it in good time, we will say so on the phone, so you can call someone else.

How long does a boiler service take, and what do you check?

About an hour. We check the burner, seals and safety devices, test the flue gases with an analyser, clean the condensate trap, check the pressure and controls, and give you a written report. If anything needs fixing, we tell you the price before we do it.

I am a landlord. What does a gas safety certificate cover?

The law asks for a gas safety check every 12 months. We check every gas appliance, flue and the ventilation, and email the certificate (often called a CP12) the same day. Give your tenants a copy within 28 days of the check, and new tenants before they move in. We send you a reminder before the next one is due.

Do you fit new boilers?

Yes. We come round for a free survey, then send a written fixed price with a choice of boilers. Most swaps take a day. We register the new boiler with Building Control through the Gas Safe Register, and the maker's warranty, often 7 to 10 years, stays valid as long as it is serviced every year.

Can I send photos instead of having someone visit?

Please do. Photos let us price most small jobs without a visit, and bring the right parts first time. Use the quote form, then attach the photos in WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer when the job is done. For a new boiler, we ask for 20% when you order, and the rest when it is fitted and working.
