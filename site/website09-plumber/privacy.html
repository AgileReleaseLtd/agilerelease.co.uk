Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Bookings and quotes

When you book a boiler service or ask for a quote, the website writes your message as a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, address, postcode or photos.

Once you send it, we see your message, your photos and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them to price the job, arrange the visit and keep a record of the work we did, such as a service report or a gas safety certificate.

How long we keep things

We keep job records, service reports and gas safety certificates for six years, in case you, or whoever owns the home next, needs them. Chats about jobs that did not go ahead are deleted after twelve months.

The postcode checker

The postcode you type into the area checker stays in your browser. It is compared with our list of areas on your own device and is not sent anywhere.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts and maps. The "Open in Google Maps" link only takes you to Google if you choose to follow it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@pinnheating.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
