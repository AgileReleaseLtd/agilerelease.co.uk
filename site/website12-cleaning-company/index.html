Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Send us a message and a person will reply.

Not sure which clean you need? Send a few photos and we'll tell you.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to be in?

No. Most customers give us a key or use a key safe. For the very first clean it helps if you're home for ten minutes to show us round, but it isn't essential.

How do you look after my keys?

Each key goes on a tag with a code, never your name or address, and lives in a locked key safe at our office. Your cleaner signs it out on the day and back in afterwards. If you stop using us, we hand it back within a week.

Can I cancel or move a clean?

Yes. Tell us at least 48 hours before and there's no charge. With less notice we charge half the visit, because your cleaner's day is planned around you. If we can't get in on the day, we charge the full visit. End-of-tenancy cleans can be moved free up to five days before.

Will I get the same cleaner every time?

Yes, for regular cleans. When your cleaner is on holiday we tell you who's coming instead, or you can skip that visit.

Do I need to provide products or a vacuum?

No. We bring everything, plant-based as standard. If you'd like us to use your own products, leave them out and tell us.

What if something gets broken?

Your cleaner tells you on the day, and we put it right. We're insured for accidental damage (see the example details above).

Do you clean around pets?

Happily. Tell us about them when you book, and whether they should be in another room while we work.

How and when do I pay?

After each clean, by card link or bank transfer. Regular customers can pay once a month instead. End-of-tenancy cleans need a £50 deposit to hold the date, which comes off the bill.

Will I get my deposit back?

Our end-of-tenancy checklist follows the order an inventory clerk checks a home, and we re-clean anything on it that's missed if you tell us within 48 hours. We can't promise the deposit itself, because that also depends on damage and wear and tear.
