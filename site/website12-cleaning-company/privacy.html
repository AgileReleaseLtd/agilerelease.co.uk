Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website doesn't collect or keep anything about you.

Estimates and booking requests

The estimate is worked out in your own browser. When you send a booking request, the website writes it as a WhatsApp message (or an email) on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, address, phone number or notes.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number or email address, like any other message. We use them to confirm the booking, to plan your clean and to contact you about it.

Photos you send us

Photos of your rooms help us confirm a price. We use them only for that, and delete them from our phones once the price is agreed. Photos we take after an end-of-tenancy clean are sent to you and kept for three months, in case your landlord or agent asks about the clean.

Keys and access

Key safe codes are only ever given by phone and never written down with your address. Keys we hold are on coded tags, kept in a locked key safe at our office.

How long we keep things

  • Messages and booking details: twelve months after your last clean.
  • Invoices: six years, because the law says so.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@tesselcleaning.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
