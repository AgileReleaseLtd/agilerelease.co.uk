How we price

The price on the phone is the price on the bill

Locksmith scams are real: a cheap advert, then a much bigger bill at the door. We work the other way round. Every step below has to line up before we start, like the pins in a lock.

Shear line Phone price, door price and bill: the same number.

  1. 1

    On the phone

    Tell us the door, the lock and your postcode. A photo on WhatsApp helps. We give you a total price with VAT, and an arrival time.

  2. 2

    Before we arrive

    We text you the locksmith's name and the price we agreed. If we're running late, we ring you to say so.

  3. 3

    At your door

    We look at the lock, check you live there, and confirm the price in writing before we start. If it has to change and you say no, you pay nothing.

  4. 4

    When we finish

    Pay by card, contactless or bank transfer. You get a receipt listing every part, and a 12-month guarantee.

Security upgrades

Is your front door lock snap-proof?

Most UPVC and composite doors are locked by a euro cylinder: the part the key goes into. Cheap ones can be snapped and the door opened in under a minute. A snap-resistant cylinder costs from £95 fitted.

Look at the face of the cylinder, by the keyhole. A TS 007 3-star or Sold Secure Diamond marking means it has been tested against snapping. No marking? It probably hasn't.

Questions

Asked on the phone every week

Something else? Call or message us. We'd rather you ask than worry.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Not in our home patch (Hornchurch, Elm Park, Upminster, Gidea Park and Romford town centre). Further out there's a travel charge of £15 or £30, and we tell you on the phone. The lock-out price includes coming out and getting you in.

How do you check I live there?

We ask for photo ID with the address on it, or photo ID and a letter or bill in your name to that address. If it's all inside, we open the door and you show us straight away, before we do anything else. The locked-out guide lists what counts.

Will you damage my door or lock?

Most doors open without damage. If a lock really has to be drilled, we tell you first and agree the price of the new lock before we drill.

Do you really work all night and on bank holidays?

Yes. The phone is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Nights (10pm to 7am) and bank holidays are charged at the night rate, which is on the price guide.

I rent. Can you change my locks?

Usually, yes, but check your tenancy agreement first: most say you need your landlord's agreement and must give them a key. After a break-in we can make the door secure straight away and change the lock once your landlord or agent agrees.

Can you get into my car?

No. We work on homes, flats and small shops. For a car, call your breakdown cover or an auto locksmith.

Is there a guarantee?

Every lock we fit is guaranteed for 12 months, parts and labour. If something we fitted goes wrong, we come back and fix it at no charge.
