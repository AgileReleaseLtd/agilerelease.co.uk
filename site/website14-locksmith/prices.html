How we price

Quoted on the phone. Agreed at the door. Nothing added after.

Locksmith scams usually work the same way: a low price to get through the door, then extras once the lock is in pieces. So we do it the other way round.

  1. On the phone we ask about the door, the lock and where you are. A photo on WhatsApp helps. You get a total price with VAT.
  2. At your door we look before we touch anything, then send the price to your phone in writing. Work starts when you say yes.
  3. If it changes (a lock that can't be picked, a broken part we couldn't see), we stop and tell you. You can say no and pay nothing.
  4. When we finish you get an itemised receipt and a 12-month guarantee on what we fitted.

  • Parking, and time spent waiting
