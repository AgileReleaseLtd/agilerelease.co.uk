Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website doesn't collect or keep anything about you.

Quotes, bookings and calls

When you use the quote form, the website writes your request as a WhatsApp message (or an email) on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, postcode, number or note.

Once you send it, or when you call us, we see your message, your number and anything you tell us, like any other call or message. We use them only to price and do the job you asked for, and to contact you about it.

When we open a door

Before we let anyone into a home, we check they live there. We write the person's name and the type of ID they showed on the job sheet, with the address and the work done. We don't copy or photograph ID. Job sheets are kept for six years for our accounts and our insurers, then destroyed.

Your postcode on this device

If you check your postcode on this website, your browser remembers the first half of it (for example RM12) so the call button can show your arrival time. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by phone, WhatsApp or email to help@nightjarlocks.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
