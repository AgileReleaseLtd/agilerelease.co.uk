        About us

        Kor means embers

        Kor is the Turkish word for the glow of charcoal once the flames die down. That is when a grill is at its hottest, and when we cook. Every skewer goes over lumpwood charcoal, the way it is done at the ocakbaşı grills of Adana and Istanbul.

        We are a family takeaway on Varley Parade, a minute from Kilburn High Road. We mince the lamb for the Adana by hand, bake the bread and lahmacun in our stone oven every evening, and build the döner fresh each morning.

        • Lumpwood charcoalNever gas, never a griddle.
        • Stone-oven breadLavash, pide and lahmacun, baked here.
        • Halal meatFrom a family butcher in Willesden.

        Questions

        Before you order

        Anything else? Message or call us.

        How long will my food take?

        Usually 25 to 55 minutes for delivery, depending on your area, and 15 to 20 minutes for collection. Friday and Saturday nights can take a little longer. We tell you the time when we confirm your order.

        How do I pay?

        Cash or card when the food arrives, or when you collect it. Our drivers carry a card machine. Nothing is paid on this website.

        Is there a minimum order?

        For delivery, yes. It depends on your area: £12 in NW6, up to £25 for the areas furthest away. Collection has no minimum.

        Can I order when you are closed?

        Yes. Your order goes in as a pre-order for when we next open. Pick the time you want it and we will confirm it when the kitchen opens.

        Is your meat halal?

        Yes. All our lamb, beef and chicken is halal, from a family butcher in Willesden.

        I have a food allergy. Can I still order?

        Please call us before you order, on 020 7946 0734, so we can talk it through. We cook everything in one small kitchen, on one grill and in one oven, so we cannot promise any dish is free from traces.

        Can I change or cancel my order?

        Call us straight away. Once the food is on the grill we cannot cancel it, but we can usually add to it.
