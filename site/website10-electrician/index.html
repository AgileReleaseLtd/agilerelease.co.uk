    C6Safety

    When something goes wrong

    Plain advice for the common scares. If in doubt, switch off at the main switch and call us.

    All our safety advice

    • Fuse box keeps tripping?

      Unplug everything on that circuit, reset the switch, then plug things back in one at a time. The one that trips it is usually the culprit.

      Step by step

    • Exposed wires or a burning smell?

      Never touch them. Switch off at the main switch if you can do it safely, keep everyone away and call us.

      What to do

    • Power cut in the street?

      If your neighbours are off too, it's the local network, not your wiring. Call 105, free, to report it and get updates.

    C7Questions

    Before you book

    Anything else? Send us a message, with a photo if it helps, and we'll answer it.

    Ask on WhatsApp

    Do you charge for quotes?

    No. Small jobs we price from photos on WhatsApp. For rewires, fuse boxes on old wiring and long EV cable runs we visit first, free, anywhere in our area.

    Do your prices include VAT?

    Yes. Every price on this website includes VAT at 20%.

    How do I pay?

    By bank transfer or card. Small jobs are paid when we finish. For a rewire we take a 20% deposit to hold the dates, then you pay in stages as the work is done.

    Do I need to be at home?

    For most jobs, yes, or you can leave us a key. For a landlord EICR your tenants can let us in, and we arrange the time with them.

    Will I need to tell building control?

    Not yourself. Some work has to be notified: a new circuit, a new fuse box, or work in a bathroom. We do that through our scheme, and the Building Regulations certificate comes to you by post.

    Can you sign off work someone else did?

    No electrician can certify work they didn't do. We can inspect it and give you an EICR, and your council's building control team can tell you how to get it approved.

    Do you do emergencies?

    We're not a 24-hour service. On working days we try to reach urgent faults the same day. Out of hours, if something looks dangerous, switch off at the main switch. For a power cut, call 105.

    How much mess does a rewire make?

    Some. We cut channels in walls and lift floorboards. We cover floors and furniture, work room by room and clear up at the end of each day. Plastering afterwards is a separate job, which we can arrange.
