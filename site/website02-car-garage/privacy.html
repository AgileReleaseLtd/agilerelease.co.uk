Privacy Your details

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Bookings and quotes

When you book or ask for a quote, the website writes your message on your own device, as a WhatsApp message or an email. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, number, registration or anything else you type.

Once you send it, we see your message and your number or email address, like any other message. We use them only to book your car in, price the work, and contact you about it.

Your car's records

When we work on your car we keep a job record: the registration, the mileage, the work done and the parts fitted. We keep it for six years, because we need it for the guarantee on our work and for our accounts. MOT results are also recorded on the DVSA's MOT database, as the law requires.

Collection and delivery

If we collect your car, we keep the address only until the car is back with you, and we note the mileage and any marks when we pick it up.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where we don't need to keep it, by WhatsApp or by email to workshop@gantrymotorworks.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
