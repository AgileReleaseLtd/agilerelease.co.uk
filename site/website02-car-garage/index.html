Bay 05 Questions

MOTs and repairs, answered

Something else? Message or call us and a mechanic will answer.

What happens if my car fails its MOT?

You get a refusal certificate listing what failed, and we give you a price to fix it. Nothing is repaired until you say yes.

If we do the repairs and retest the car here within 10 working days, the retest is free. If you take the car away, bring it back within 10 working days for a partial retest at £22.50. Some small items, like bulbs, wipers and tyres, are retested free if you bring the car back by the end of the next working day.

You can only drive a failed car away if your old MOT has not run out and nothing was marked "dangerous". Otherwise it has to be fixed first.

How early can I get my MOT done?

Up to a month (minus a day) before it runs out, and you keep the same renewal date. If it runs out on 15 May, the earliest is 16 April. Your due date is on your last certificate, or on GOV.UK's MOT history checker.

How long does an MOT take? Can I wait?

The test takes 45 minutes to an hour. Morning drop-offs are ready by 1pm and afternoon drop-offs by 5:30pm. You're welcome to wait for an MOT: there's coffee and Wi-Fi in the waiting room.

My MOT has run out. Can I drive the car to you?

Yes, to an MOT that is already booked, as long as the car is roadworthy. Book first and keep the booking message on your phone. Or ask us to collect it.

Will servicing here affect my new-car warranty?

No. Any garage can service a car under warranty, as long as the service follows the maker's schedule and the parts match the original quality. We do both, and stamp your service book or give you a printed record.

Will you do work I haven't agreed to?

Never. If we find something, we send you a price and a photo, and wait for your answer. If it's a safety problem we'll explain why, but it's still your choice.

Do you work on hybrids and electric cars?

Hybrids, yes: servicing, MOTs and most repairs. On fully electric cars we do MOTs, tyres, brakes, suspension and air conditioning. Faults in the battery or motor go to a specialist, and we'll tell you who.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay when you collect the car, or by bank transfer if we deliver it back. There's no deposit for MOTs or servicing.
