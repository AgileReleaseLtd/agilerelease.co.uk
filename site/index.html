What we do

Product engineering and consultancy from a team that also ships its own software.

Product engineering, end to end

From a blank repository to a live, monitored service. We take on discovery, architecture, build, release and the unglamorous operational work that keeps software running — and we do it in small, shippable increments so you see progress every week, not every quarter.

Technical consultancy

Architecture reviews, delivery rescues and a straight answer on the build-vs-buy call. We embed with your team and leave it stronger than we found it.

Mobile & apps

Android and cross-platform apps — designed, published and maintained. We run our own portfolio of games, so we know the store lifecycle first-hand.

Cloud & platform

Deployment pipelines, infrastructure and observability that a small team can actually operate — no cloud bill surprises.

Integrations & APIs

Third-party and government APIs, wired up properly. We built a full HMRC Making Tax Digital integration for EasyTaxer — compliance-grade integration is our comfort zone.

EasyTaxer

HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital software

Our flagship product. EasyTaxer helps UK sole traders and landlords meet their Making Tax Digital for Income Tax obligations — recording income and expenses, calculating tax in real time, and submitting quarterly updates and final declarations directly to HMRC. It's a live, revenue-generating SaaS platform we designed, built and run in-house.
