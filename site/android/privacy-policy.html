Privacy Policy — Agile Release Games

Effective 7 July 2026 · Last updated 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games published by Agile Release Limited ("Agile Release", "we", "us") on Google Play (each, "the Game") — including 2048 Cosmos, Word Search, and our other Android titles. By using one of our Games you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • Our Games do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your progress (scores, levels, settings) is stored locally on your device; if you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google Play Games Saved Games (cloud save, held by Google — we never receive it).
  • Third-party Google services we use — ads, Google Play Games, and in-app purchases — collect limited data as described below.
  • We run no crash-reporting SDK of our own. Some Games use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Games are played (see 2.5), subject to your consent in the EEA and UK.

1. Who we are

These Games are published by Agile Release Limited, a company registered in England and Wales, registered office: Agile Release Limited, 7 Larkshall Road, London, E4 7HS, United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, contact info@agilerelease.co.uk.

2. What data the Games process

2.1 Game progress and settings (not sent to us)

Game progress and settings (saves, best scores, streaks, unlocked content, sound/tutorial preferences, coins and rewards) are stored locally via Android app storage and removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Cloud save (optional, Google Play Games). If you sign in to Google Play Games, the Game also keeps a copy of that progress in your Google Play Games Saved Games, so it can follow you to another device. The copy is held by Google under your Google Play Games account and governed by Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive it. If you never sign in, nothing is saved there.

2.2 Leaderboards & achievements (Google Play Games Services, optional)

Our Games offer optional leaderboards and achievements via Google Play Games Services. If you choose to sign in, Google associates your gameplay (best scores, achievements) with your Google Play Games player ID so those features work. Signing in is optional — every Game is fully playable without it, and if you never sign in, no leaderboard/achievement data is collected. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy; manage or delete it in your Google Play Games settings.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our Games are free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect your advertising identifier, device and network information, app interactions, and general (IP-based) location. See Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): we show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before personalised ads load.
  • Your controls: reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Removing ads: where offered, a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase disables ads in that Game.

2.4 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (e.g. Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details; the app only records, on your device, what you have purchased so it can unlock and restore content.

2.5 Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase, some Games)

Some Games (currently Word Search, Killer Sudoku, Mahjong Solitaire, 2048 Cosmos, 2048 Drop, Roost, Coin Sort, Color by Number, Starbit, Block Puzzle, Hexodus, Cosmos Merge, Cosmos Nonograms, Word Connect, Jigsaw Puzzles, Trinket, Star Blast, Pipe Connect, Tap Away, Color Loader, Bus Depot, Water Sort and Curve Soccer) use Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, to tell us how the Games are played: for example, how many players come back the next day, which levels are too hard, and whether hints are used. It records:

  • Gameplay events: a level started, completed or failed, with the level number, the difficulty level (in Games that have one), the time taken in seconds, the number of moves (in some Games), the number of hints and whether it was a clean clear; the daily puzzle completed; a hint or another in-game help (such as a shuffle, undo, reveal or power-up) used; in Games without levels, a game started or finished with its score; in idle Games, a reset, a boost or offline earnings collected; the tutorial finished; and your chosen colour theme.
  • Automatic usage data: first open, sessions, app version, device model, operating system, language, and in-app purchases made through Google Play.
  • An app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for this installation, and the approximate location (country or city) that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address.

We do not send your name, email, Google account, Play Games player ID or any free text. Analytics in our Games does not read your Advertising ID. We have turned off Google signals and ads personalisation, so this data is not used for advertising.

  • Consent (EEA/UK): analytics stays off until you answer the same Google consent form (UMP) used for ads, and it runs only if you consent. If you decline, no analytics data is sent. Outside the EEA and UK it runs on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the Games.
  • Changing your choice: use Privacy options in the Game's Settings.
  • Retention: event data is kept for 2 months and then deleted by Google Analytics.

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise GPS location, or biometric data. We run no crash-reporting SDK, and no analytics other than the Google Analytics for Firebase described in 2.5.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with Google — AdMob (advertising), Google Play Billing (purchases), Google Play Games Services (leaderboards, achievements and cloud save), and Google Analytics for Firebase (game analytics, in some Games). For Analytics, Google acts as our data processor; for the other services it acts under its own privacy policy. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the service.

5. Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data — remains until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.
  • Leaderboard/achievement data — held by Google Play Games under Google's retention policies; manage or delete it in your Play Games settings, or email us.
  • Cloud save data — held by Google Play Games until you delete it in your Play Games settings (delete your Play Games profile and its data).
  • Advertising data — governed by Google's retention policies.
  • Analytics data (Google Analytics for Firebase) — event data is deleted automatically after 2 months. To ask us to delete the analytics data for your installation sooner, email us. We can delete it by app-instance ID, which the Game can show on request.

To request deletion of data associated with our Games, see our Data Deletion Request page or email info@agilerelease.co.uk.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (UK/EEA under UK GDPR/GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, etc.) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your personal data. As most data is on your device or held by Google, the fastest route is to clear the app's data / uninstall or use your Google account controls. For anything else, contact info@agilerelease.co.uk. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

7. Children

The Games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; where required, ad personalisation is disabled for users indicated as children.

8. International transfers

Google's services may process data outside the UK/EEA under appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in Google's privacy documentation.
