    How paying works

    There is no payment on this website. When your order arrives, we reply with a secure card payment link from our card machine provider, or take payment over the phone if you prefer. Printed cards can also be paid for at the desk.

    We email the e-voucher, or put the card out for you, once it is paid. Your voucher number is on it.

    The small print

    Voucher terms

    • Vouchers are valid for 12 months from the day they are paid for. The date is printed on the voucher.
    • Amount vouchers can be spent on any treatment, over as many visits as you like, until the balance runs out.
    • Treatment vouchers cover that treatment, even if its price goes up.
    • To use one, mention the voucher number when you book. Patch tests still apply for tints, lifts and lash extensions.
    • Vouchers cannot be swapped for cash. If an e-voucher goes missing, we will resend it.
    • Changed your mind? We refund unused vouchers within 14 days of buying them.
