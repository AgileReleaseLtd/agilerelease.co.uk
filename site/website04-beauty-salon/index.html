Good to know

Before you book

Three things we ask of everyone. They keep treatments safe and the diary fair.

Patch tests

Lash and brow tints, lash lifts, brow lamination and lash extensions all need a patch test at least 48 hours before your appointment.

It is free and takes five minutes. Pop in any time we are open; no appointment needed. We repeat it every six months, and sooner if you have reacted to anything, changed medication or become pregnant.

Please tell us if you have ever reacted to hair dye or a black henna tattoo. No patch test means no treatment: a reaction near the eyes can be serious, so we do not make exceptions.

Cancelling or moving

Message us at least 48 hours before to cancel or move your appointment, and there is no charge.

With less notice we may charge half the treatment price, and the full price if you do not come at all. That pays for a slot we could have given to someone else.

If you are ill, tell us as soon as you can. We will be reasonable.

Deposits and running late

Lash extension full sets, and bookings over £100, need a £20 deposit. It comes off your bill, and we send a secure card payment link when we confirm.

Running late? Message us. If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten your treatment, so the next person is not kept waiting.

    Questions

    Asked at the desk

    Anything else? Message us and we will reply.

    How do I book, and how do I know it is confirmed?

    Choose your treatments and a time on the booking page, then send the request on WhatsApp or by email. Nothing is booked until we reply to confirm, usually within the hour while we are open. You can also simply call us.

    Do I need a patch test?

    Yes, for lash and brow tints, lash lifts, brow lamination and lash extensions. It is free, takes five minutes and must be at least 48 hours before your appointment. Pop in any time we are open. More about patch tests.

    How long do gel and BIAB last?

    Gel polish lasts about two weeks on hands and three to four weeks on toes. BIAB lasts three to four weeks, then needs an infill or a removal. Please do not peel either off: it takes layers of your own nail with it. Nail aftercare.

    What should I do before a wax?

    Let the hair grow to about 5 mm, roughly four weeks after shaving. Exfoliate gently a day or two before, and skip body lotion on the day. Tell us if you use retinoids or acne medication, as we may not be able to wax that area.

    Can I book if I am pregnant?

    Most treatments, yes, so please tell us when you book. Some facial treatments are best avoided, we will help you get comfortable on the couch, and hormones can make waxing more sensitive and lash lifts less predictable. If in doubt, check with your midwife.

    Is there a minimum age?

    We do not do lash extensions, tints, lifts or intimate waxing for anyone under 16. Sixteen and seventeen year olds need a parent or guardian with them for those. Nails are fine from 12 with an adult.

    How can I pay?

    Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash, at the end of your appointment. Gift vouchers are taken at the desk: just mention the voucher when you book.
