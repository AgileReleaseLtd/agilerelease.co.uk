      04 Good to know

      Before you book.

      A few house rules. They keep colour safe, the diary fair and the next person's appointment on time.

      Our colour rule

      A skin test, at least 48 hours before colour.

      If you are new to colour with us, or your last colour here was more than six months ago, we do a quick skin test first. Colour can cause a reaction even if you have had it for years, so this is our rule for everyone.

      1. Any day we are openThe skin testFree, and five minutes. Pop in without an appointment and we put a dab of colour behind your ear.
      2. Then wait48 hoursCarry on as normal. If it itches, stings or goes red, wash it off and tell us.
      3. Two days later or afterYour colourThe booking page only offers times at least 48 hours after a test you can make.

      This covers every colour service, including toners and grey blending. Pregnant, on new medication, or had a tattoo or black henna recently? Tell us, as we may need to test again.

      Moving or cancelling

      Give us 48 hours' notice and it costs nothing. With less notice, we may charge half the price of the services you booked. If we fill the chair, we won't.

      Deposits

      Appointments of three hours or more need a £30 deposit when we confirm. It comes off your bill, and you get it back if you give us 48 hours' notice to cancel.

      Running late

      Message us. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten the service or move you, so the next person is not kept waiting.

      Children

      Under-12s can book a child's cut in the salon or a kids' clipper cut with the barbers. We do not colour hair for anyone under 16.

      05 Questions

      Asked at the desk.

      Anything else? Send us a message and we will reply.

      Do you take walk-ins?

      The barbers do, Monday to Wednesday, when a chair is free. Message us first and we will tell you the wait. The salon side is by appointment.

      Do you cut Afro and textured hair?

      Yes. Femi cuts and fades every hair type on the barbers' side, and Nadia specialises in curls and coils. Tell us a little about your hair when you book.

      How long will I be in the chair?

      The booking page adds it up for you. As a guide, a cut and blow-dry takes an hour, and a balayage on long hair about three and a half hours.

      Can two of us come at the same time?

      Yes. Send a request for each person and say so in the note. We will put you next to each other if we can.

      How do I pay?

      By card, phone or cash when you leave. Tips are welcome but never expected.

      What if I am not happy with my hair?

      Tell us within seven days and we will put it right, free of charge.

      Is the salon step-free?

      Yes, from the pavement to the basins, and the toilet is on the ground floor.
