    Green waste

    Taken away properly, and composted

    Clippings, leaves and branches go in our own vans to a licensed composting site, not a lay-by. We're registered with the Environment Agency as an upper-tier waste carrier, because on landscaping jobs we also take away old slabs, soil and fence panels.

    If you pay anyone to take rubbish away, the law expects you to check they're a registered waste carrier. If your waste is fly-tipped, you can be fined too. You can check any carrier on the Environment Agency's public register.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Anything else? Send us a message and we'll reply the same day.

    Do I need to be in when you come?

    No, as long as we can get into the garden: an unlocked side gate, a key safe or a code is fine. If the only way in is through the house, someone needs to be home, and we bring dust sheets and boot covers.

    What happens when it rains?

    We work in light rain. In heavy rain, frost or snow we don't walk on lawns or dig wet soil, because it does more harm than good. We move the visit and tell you on WhatsApp.

    Will you cut my hedge in spring or summer?

    Yes, after we've checked it for nesting birds. Any part with an active nest is left until the young have flown, which the law requires. If the hedge needs a big cut back, we book it for winter.

    Do you take on tree work?

    We prune small trees and fruit trees from the ground or a ladder, up to about four metres. For bigger trees we'll recommend a qualified tree surgeon. Carshalton village and some nearby streets are conservation areas, where most tree work needs six weeks' notice to Sutton Council first, and trees with a preservation order need consent.

    Is there a contract for a visit plan?

    No. You pay monthly by standing order and can stop with a month's notice. If you're away, we can pause visits or keep going as normal.

    Do you bring everything you need?

    Yes: tools, mowers, bags and the van to take green waste away. We use battery mowers and trimmers in most gardens, which keeps the noise down for your neighbours. We'll ask to use an outside tap for watering in new plants.

    How do I pay?

    Visit plans by monthly standing order. One-off jobs by bank transfer within seven days of the invoice. For landscaping over £1,000 we ask for a quarter up front when we order materials, and the rest when the job is finished.
