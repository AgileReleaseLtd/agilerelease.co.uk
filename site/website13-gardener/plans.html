      Good to know

      How plans work

      • Starting with a catch-up

        If the garden has been left for a while, the first visit is a one-off tidy to get it back in shape. We quote for that separately, then the plan keeps it there.

      • Weather and holidays

        We don't mow wet lawns or dig frozen soil, so we move visits in bad weather and tell you on WhatsApp. Going away? We can pause, or water while you're gone.

      • What's quoted separately

        Hard cutting back of overgrown hedges, big clearances, tree work above ladder height and landscaping. Weekly and fortnightly plans include two hedge trims a year.
