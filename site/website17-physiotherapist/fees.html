Packages

Pay up front and save. Buy a package at the clinic, then use it at any appointment of that type.

Health insurance

Claiming on health insurance

Many private health insurance policies cover physiotherapy, and some cover sports massage. Here is how it works, step by step.

Insurers we can bill direct Examples

    Example names only. A real clinic lists the insurers it is registered with. Not on the list? You can usually still pay us and claim it back.

    Health cash plans Examples

      With a cash plan, you pay us on the day and claim back part or all of the cost.

      1. Call your insurer first

        Before your first appointment, tell them you want physiotherapy. Ask for a pre-authorisation code and how many sessions it covers.

      2. Check your excess and limits

        Ask how much your excess is, and whether there is a yearly limit for therapies. Some policies need a GP referral first.

      3. Book and tell us

        When you book, choose Health insurance and give your insurer and authorisation code. Bring your membership number to your first visit.

      4. Pay or we invoice

        Depending on your insurer, we send the invoice to them, or you pay us and claim it back. Both are explained below.

      Route A

      We invoice your insurer

      For insurers we are registered with, we send our invoices to them after each appointment. You pay nothing on the day, apart from any excess your insurer asks you to pay to us.

      If your insurer pays less than our fee, or your authorised sessions run out, we tell you before we charge you anything.

      Route B

      You pay, then claim it back

      You pay us by card after each appointment. We email an itemised invoice with your practitioner’s registration details, and you send it to your insurer or cash plan.

      Your insurer pays you back, less any excess, up to the limits of your policy.

      Insurers do not pay for missed appointments or late cancellation fees. Treatment beyond what your insurer has authorised is charged at our normal prices, and we always check with you first.

      Cancellations

      Cancellation policy

      A late cancellation usually means someone in pain could have had that appointment. So we ask for notice, and keep it fair.

      24 hours

      Free to move or cancel

      Tell us by WhatsApp, phone or email at least 24 hours before, and there is nothing to pay.

      50%

      Less than 24 hours’ notice

      We charge half the appointment fee. For a package, it uses half an appointment.

      100%

      Missed without telling us

      We charge the full fee, or it uses a whole appointment from your package.

      Ill, or something urgent came up? Tell us as soon as you can. We waive the fee the first time, and we never charge if the clinic has to cancel.
