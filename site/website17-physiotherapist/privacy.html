Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you. Your health records are kept safely at the clinic, as the law requires.

Booking on this website

When you book, the website writes your request as a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, your note or your number.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them to arrange your appointment and to contact you about it. We delete booking chats after twelve months.

Your health records

At your first appointment we start a clinical record: what you tell us, what we find and the treatment we agree. Physiotherapists must keep clinical records, and we keep adult records for eight years after your last appointment, in a secure system that only the clinical team can open.

We only share your record with others, such as your GP, your insurer or a specialist, when you agree or when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we do not have to keep, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@spindlephysio.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Spindle Physio is a made-up business on a sample website, so this notice is an example of what a small clinic’s privacy page could say. A real clinic’s notice would be checked against its own record keeping.
