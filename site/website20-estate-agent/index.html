No. 6 Questions

Things people ask us

Anything else? Send us a message and we will reply the same day.

How much does it cost to sell with you?

1.44% of the sale price including VAT (1.2% + VAT), paid only when the sale completes. There is nothing to pay upfront. If you need an EPC, we book one at cost. You can end the agreement with two weeks' notice after the first eight weeks.

What do "under offer" and "sold STC" mean?

Under offer: the seller has accepted an offer, but you can still view and make your own offer. Sold STC (subject to contract): the sale is going through and contracts are not yet exchanged. We keep both on the website until completion, so you can see what is moving.

Do you charge tenants any fees?

No. Tenants pay the rent, a refundable deposit and, if they choose to go ahead, a refundable holding deposit of one week's rent. The law allows a few other charges in specific cases, which we list on our fees page.

How do viewings work?

Pick a time on the property's page and send the request on WhatsApp. We confirm by message, usually within the hour. One of us always comes with you, including evenings until 7:30pm and Saturdays.

Is the rough guide a valuation?

No. It is a range based on what we have sold and let nearby, so you know roughly where you stand. A valuation is a visit: we look at the condition, light, outside space and, for flats, the lease and service charge, then give you one figure in writing.

Can tenants have pets?

Since 1 May 2026, tenants can ask to keep a pet and landlords must consider the request and give a reason if they say no. Many of our landlords say yes, and we note which homes welcome pets on each listing.

How long does it take to let a home?

Most homes we market in Bow are let within two to three weeks. References and Right to Rent checks then take three to five working days.

Do you cover my street?

If you are in E3, almost certainly. We also cover the E3 side of Victoria Park and the edges of Hackney Wick and Poplar. Ask us if you are not sure.
