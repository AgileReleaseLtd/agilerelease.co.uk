Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you.

Viewings, valuations and questions

When you book a viewing or ask for a valuation, the website writes your request as a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and the website itself never receives or stores your name, address or note.

Once you send it, we see your message and your WhatsApp number, like any other message. We use them to arrange the viewing or valuation and to follow it up. We delete enquiry chats after twelve months unless you become a client.

If you become a client

Sellers, buyers, landlords and tenants give us more information, such as identity documents for money laundering checks and references for tenancies. We keep these for as long as the law requires (usually five or six years) and explain this in writing when you sign up.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts. The search page remembers your last search in this browser tab only, so the Back link works. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by WhatsApp or by email to hello@transom.example. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
