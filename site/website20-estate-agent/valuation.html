This range comes from homes we have sold and let in Bow, Old Ford and Mile End over the last year. It cannot see your home: light, outside space, the street and, for flats, the lease and service charge all move the price. The visit gives you one figure you can rely on.

No. 1 How it works

From valuation to keys

  1. 1

    We visit

    About 40 minutes. We look at every room, the outside space and any paperwork you have, such as the lease or planning approvals.

  2. 2

    You get it in writing

    Within 24 hours: our figure, the homes we compared it with, our fee and how we would market it.

  3. 3

    We get it ready

    Photos, a floor plan, the EPC and the material information buyers or tenants need, checked with you before anything goes live.

  4. 4

    We see it through

    Accompanied viewings, honest feedback, offers in writing, and a weekly update until completion or move-in day.
