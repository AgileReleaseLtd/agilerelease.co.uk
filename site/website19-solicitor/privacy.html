Privacy

Your details

Short version: this website does not collect or keep anything about you. What you send us, we use only to help you.

Enquiries

When you use the enquiry form, the website writes your message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app, and the website itself never receives or stores what you type.

Once you send it, we see your message and your phone number or email address. We use them to reply, and to check that we can act for you. If you do not become a client, we delete the enquiry after twelve months.

If you become a client

We keep your file, and the identity checks the money laundering rules require, for as long as the law and our insurers expect: usually at least six years after your matter ends. We keep original wills and lasting powers of attorney safe until they are needed.

We share your details only with people who need them for your matter, such as HM Land Registry, the court, HMRC, your lender, the other side's lawyers and our identity check provider. We never sell them.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other websites, including fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by email to hello@quinlanmensah.example or by letter to our office. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
