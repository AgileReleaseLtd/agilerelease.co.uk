Keep it short

Please don't send confidential details in your first message: no account numbers, no copies of documents, and nothing about the other side you would not want them to see. We'll ask for what we need once we have agreed to act for you.

We are not your solicitors yet

Sending an enquiry does not create a solicitor and client relationship. We first check we can act (for example, that we don't already act for someone on the other side), confirm your identity and send our terms. We only act for you once you have agreed them. Until then, please don't rely on anything we say as legal advice.
